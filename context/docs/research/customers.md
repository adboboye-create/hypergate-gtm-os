## 3. Case studies in detail

### 3.1 Jefferies (investment bank, USA)

- Original: Google Android Enterprise blog, "Investment bank Jefferies mobilizes teams with Android", author Eugene Yeh, Head of Growth, Android Enterprise Business; dated September 16, 2019 per the fetched Google page (source: https://blog.google/products/android-enterprise/jefferies-case-study/, checked 2026-11-13).
- Republished by Hypergate as "Success Story with Jefferies" (page metadata datePublished 2019-09-10), author shown as Lukas Schönbächler (source: https://hypergate.com/blog/success-story-jefferies/, checked 2026-11-13).
- Context (Hypergate page): "Jefferies is a U.S financial services organization and investment bank with 5000 Employees", "Listed as a Fortune 500 company", "85% of their work devices are employee-owned (BYOD)" (source: https://hypergate.com/blog/success-story-jefferies/, checked 2026-11-13).
- Trigger / need: "senior bankers frequently travel yet still require access to in-house finance systems while away from the office. Transitioning to Android Enterprise enabled the BYOD fleet with single sign-on (SSO) to secure access to corporate resources" (source: https://hypergate.com/blog/success-story-jefferies/, checked 2026-11-13).
- Hypergate's role: "When it comes to accessing legacy apps, Android Enterprise works alongside a solution from Hypergate that provides on-premise SSO authentication to Jefferies' Windows authentication infrastructure. This allows Jefferies to support a hybrid cloud environment and ensure that the user experience is seamless; employees can use the native Google Chrome app on Android to securely access internal websites wherever they are in the world." (source: https://hypergate.com/blog/success-story-jefferies/, checked 2026-11-13)
- Stack: Android work profile (BYOD), managed Google Play, Chrome. EMM not named (source: https://blog.google/products/android-enterprise/jefferies-case-study/, checked 2026-11-13).
- Outcomes (qualitative, no figures): increased user adoption, reduced IT training demands, "a quicker onboarding process (requiring only minutes to set up the device), to fewer tickets submitted by users" (source: https://hypergate.com/blog/success-story-jefferies/, checked 2026-11-13).
- Quotes, Mittul Mehta, vice president, platform security engineering at Jefferies:
  - "The Android work profile means we can provide secure access to corporate data and apps for the large number of our employees who use their personal devices at work," (source: https://hypergate.com/blog/success-story-jefferies/, checked 2026-11-13)
  - "[Users are now able to] complete their work on their phone in a fast, effective and secure way," ... "while getting reassurance that the work profile data separation means that their personal data stays private." (source: https://hypergate.com/blog/success-story-jefferies/, checked 2026-11-13)
  - Note: neither Mehta quote mentions Hypergate by name; they are about Android work profile.
- Homepage links to this study as "Read the Google Case Study" (source: https://hypergate.com/, checked 2026-11-13).

### 3.2 Maersk Drilling (offshore drilling, Denmark)

- Page metadata datePublished 2024-03-25; author shown only as an obfuscated email (source: https://hypergate.com/blog/success-story-with-maersk-drilling/, checked 2026-11-13).
- Context: "More than 2500 employees work at Maersk Drilling"; "The rigs are operated 24 hours, so employees work in shifts, and devices are shared by multiple users. The devices log out automatically and close all open sessions in the background after each shift, interrupting the workflow. The rigs are equipped with a local active directory to make sure users can authenticate even when the mobile connection is lost due to bad weather conditions." (source: https://hypergate.com/blog/success-story-with-maersk-drilling/, checked 2026-11-13)
- Problem: "each user was prompted for their credentials for each application they launched on their Android phones. Android Enterprise natively does not support Kerberos Authentication, so therefore a Single Sign-on experience with their local active directories was not possible." (same source)
- EMM: not named.
- Benefits listed (no numbers): "Improved efficiency & productivity on the rigs", "Less support due to one-time logging", "Increased user experience and convenience" (same source).
- Testimonial, Lukas Landsberg, Project Manager Business Performance, Maersk Drilling: "The Hypergate team was nice and very efficient to work with. Especially in the rollout stage, they were available even on short notice and always found solutions to upcoming challenges. I can recommend the Hypergate Solution and the Team" (source: https://hypergate.com/blog/success-story-with-maersk-drilling/, checked 2026-11-13)

### 3.3 York Regional Police (law enforcement, Canada)

- Page metadata datePublished 2024-04-10; author shown only as an obfuscated email (source: https://hypergate.com/blog/success-story-with-york-regional-police/, checked 2026-11-13).
- Context: "York Regional Police is one of the largest law enforcement organisations in Canada"; "More than 1600 officers and 600 civilians"; "Within a highly mobile work environment, both sworn officers and civilian members are using Android phones for their daily work." (same source)
- Problem: "Android Enterprise doesn't support Kerberos Authentication natively, so therefore enablement of Single Sign-On with a local active directory previously was not possible." (same source)
- EMM: MobileIron (named in the quote below).
- Benefits listed (no numbers): "Greatly reduced support due to Single Sign-On", "Increased operational security through Kerberos authentication", "Increased user experience and convenience" (same source).
- Testimonial, Pierre Hanlon, Security Administrator, York Regional Police: "Hypergate is easy to install and configure, and it seamlessly integrates into our existing MobileIron MDM environment" (source: https://hypergate.com/blog/success-story-with-york-regional-police/, checked 2026-11-13)
