# Decision needed: outbound in partner regions

For: Hypergate sales lead. Prepared 2026-11-13. Status: **awaiting decision**.

## The question

Hypergate is starting signal-based outbound: we contact named IT staff at companies showing a buying trigger, such as Acronis Cyber Files reaching end of life or Microsoft disabling NTLM. Many of these accounts sit in countries where a reseller partner is listed. **When an account is in a partner's region, who contacts it?**

## What we know

- "Hypergate is distributed through our partner network, where available." (https://hypergate.com/partners/, checked 2026-11-13)
- 28 partners are listed. They cover CH, DE, AT, UK, NL, BE, the Nordics, Eastern Europe, the US, the UAE, India, Turkey, Brazil, Israel, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Australia and New Zealand (`context/docs/research/company.md` §7). Most target markets have at least one partner.
- There is a partner portal (https://hypergate.com/partnerportal/), so a deal registration process may already exist. Unknown.
- Pricing, margins and partner contract terms are not public. Unknown.

## Options

| | A. Direct, notify partner | B. Hand to partner | C. Co-branded |
|---|---|---|---|
| How it works | Hypergate runs outreach. When a lead is positive, it is registered to the partner for that region | Partner-region accounts are passed to the partner as a lead list with the signal; the partner reaches out | Joint outreach, with Hypergate and partner both named, sent from Hypergate infrastructure |
| Speed to launch | Fast | Slow: depends on each partner | Slow: needs per-partner sign-off |
| Message quality and testing | Full control; weekly learning loop works | Lost: no visibility of copy or results | Shared control |
| Partner relationship risk | Medium: partners may see it as competing | Low | Low |
| Feedback data | Complete | Partial: depends on partners reporting back | Complete |
| Best when | Partners mainly fulfil and support, and do little prospecting themselves | Partners actively prospect and own territory | A few strategic partners want joint pipeline |

A mix is possible. For example: Option A by default, Option B for regions where a named partner actively prospects, and Option C pilots with one or two partners.

## What we need from you

1. Do any partners hold exclusive territory, or have deal registration rules that outbound must respect?
2. Which partners actively prospect, and which mainly resell or fulfil?
3. Choose A, B, C or a mix, and name any exception regions.
4. When outbound finds a positive reply in a partner region, who owns the account, and does the partner get a margin on it?
5. Is there a current list of partner-registered accounts we must not contact?

Until you decide, outbound in partner regions stays **direct, and every account is tagged with its partner region in the CRM** so it can be re-routed later. No sends happen before this decision and the compliance sign-off.
