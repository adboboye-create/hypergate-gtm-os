# Hypergate brand voice

Status: v0.1, 2026-11-13. Built only from `context/docs/research/voice.md` (exact quotes from Hypergate's own channels) unless noted. Visual rules (logo, colours, type): not researched, unknown.

## Tone of voice

What Hypergate sounds like today (all INFERRED from voice.md §9):

- Two voices. Older product pages are benefit-led and generic ("Win Across The Board", "Unmatched Usability"). Newer copy and the 2026 blog are sharper, built on one tension: usability vs control, cloud vs on-prem, framed as data sovereignty.
- The 2026 blog is the most specific voice: technical, practical, written for IT admins and mobile engineers, naming Microsoft deadlines and giving code.
- Two promises repeat everywhere: no new servers, works with what you have; and the phone works like your Windows PC.
- Swiss, security-first, conservative buyers.

How we write for Hypergate (our rules, drawn from the above):

- Lead with the specific, technical voice of the 2026 blog, not the older generic pages.
- Name the real thing: Kerberos, domain controllers, the EMM, the Microsoft KB number.
- Plain claims, no superlatives without proof.
- No em dashes (house rule; the site uses them, voice.md §9).
- Fix the site's typos and pick one spelling style; the site mixes British and American (voice.md §9). Spelling choice: to confirm with Hypergate.
- Use "seamless" sparingly; it is on almost every page (voice.md §5, §9).
